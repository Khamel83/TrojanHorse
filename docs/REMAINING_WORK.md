# Remaining Work After Ingestion

Status date: 2026-09-07
Evidence basis: [`status.json`](../work-corpus/corpus/reports/status.json),
generated by the latest local acceptance run, plus the organization and
reconciliation ledgers in `work-corpus/state/`.

## The current boundary

The source-backed evidence base, deterministic organization pass, and approved
unified-corpus first pass are complete for the currently captured local
sources. The former 68 grouped exception rows are closed by policy. Optional
downstream refinement can improve canonical identities or meeting associations,
but it is not required to make the captured corpus searchable.

These terms are intentionally separate:

- **Ingested** means the source is preserved, inventoried, represented in the
  local database, and searchable.
- **Organized** means deterministic rules produced source-backed links,
  explicit entities, task proposals, and review items.
- **Reviewed** means a human accepted or rejected an interpretation. The
  organizer does not claim this state for ambiguous records.
- **First-pass triage** means the approved policy handled a grouped queue without
  changing raw files, source classifications, or canonical facts. It records
  generic topic labels instead of inventing identities and does not claim that
  every derived interpretation is a canonical fact.
